## Structured paper notes

Record research problem, contribution, method, dataset, baselines, metrics, main results, ablations, limitations, relevance, inspected sections, and reading depth. Label statements `AUTHOR_CLAIM`, `DIRECT_EVIDENCE`, or `REVIEWER_INFERENCE`.

## Evidence synthesis

Compare agreement, disagreement, method families, dataset differences, evaluation differences, and limitations. Assign `CONSISTENT_EVIDENCE`, `MIXED_EVIDENCE`, or `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.
